Your home, your order

Everything is where you put it — and moves where you want it.

Group apps into folders or plain text titles. Then long‑press and drag anything into place: folders, the apps inside them, the dock, the progress rings, even the clock, date and weather.

Folders or text

Show categories as tidy folder tiles side by side, or as quiet text headings. Switch anytime in Settings — your order carries over.

Drag to rearrange

A long press lifts any tile; the rest glide out of the way and settle into place. The same gesture works across every surface on the home screen.

All apps, one swipe

Swipe up for the full A–Z drawer with a fast‑scroll rail. Long‑press an app to drag it straight into a home folder.

Universal search

One field finds apps, contacts and the web — and solves math inline. Type “12*7” and read the answer without leaving home.

App details

Tap through to any app's screen‑time chart, storage and data footprint, and today's usage against your daily goal.

Hidden apps

Tuck away the apps you'd rather not see, and bring them back whenever you like from a dedicated Settings list.

A board of widgets

Small tools that each do one thing well.

Build a widget board that's yours — reorder it in edit mode, remove what you don't use. Everything runs on‑device unless it truly needs the network (just weather).

Focus mode & usage goals

Start a Focus session and Minimalista quietly holds distractions back — optionally hiding alerts and silencing the device — while a live countdown ticks under your clock. Set daily limits per app and watch your screen time land where you meant it to.

  • Timed Focus sessions with an allow‑list of apps.

Private by default

Your phone is yours. We hold none of it.

No sign‑up, no ads, no advertising trackers, and no servers of our own. Your settings, folders, notes and tasks never leave the device. The only things that go out are a weather lookup and anonymous crash reports — nothing tied to you.

  • On‑device storageEverything is stored privately on your phone.
  • No accounts, no adsNothing to log into. Nothing selling your attention.
  • Optional permissionsWeather, contacts, calendar — each powers one feature, and each is optional.
